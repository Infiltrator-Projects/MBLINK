# Code of Conduct

MBLINK is an open-source engineering project. Contributors, users and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces professional, constructive and focused on the work.

## Expected conduct

Be respectful of other participants and their time. Discuss technical ideas on their merits. Give actionable feedback, assume good faith where reasonable, and clearly distinguish verified facts from hypotheses or unverified vehicle data.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Engineering discussions

Disagreement is welcome when it improves the project. Reviews should explain the technical reason for a requested change. Repeatedly derailing discussions, misrepresenting test results, or knowingly presenting unverified diagnostic definitions as verified is unacceptable.

Automotive diagnostic work can affect real vehicles. Safety concerns, uncertain provenance and incomplete validation must be raised plainly and without pressure to conceal them.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project documentation and other spaces operated for MBLINK. It also applies when someone is representing the project in an official capacity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its participants.

For conduct concerns that should not be raised publicly, contact the project maintainer privately at `infiltratr@yandex.com`. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour, its impact, any pattern of prior conduct and the need to keep the project safe and productive.
